# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

In short: we are committed to a harassment-free, welcoming, and respectful
experience for everyone who participates in this project, regardless of
background or identity.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the project maintainers at **conduct@truenas.com**. All
complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers
are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any
incident.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant 2.1, including the enforcement
guidelines, is available at the link above.
